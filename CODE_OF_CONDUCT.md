# Code of Conduct

Fors Corp projects are small, and most contributions come from a handful of people. The bar is simple: be direct about the work and decent to the people.

## Expected

- Argue about code, designs and decisions on their merits, with evidence.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Treat review feedback as information about the change, not about you, and give it the same way.
- Keep discussions on the issue or PR where others can follow them.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language in any channel tied to these projects.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome attention after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour: derailing threads, spamming, sabotaging work.

## Enforcement

Report problems to developer@marcfors.com. Reports are handled privately by the organization owner. Outcomes range from a warning to removal from the organization and its repositories, at the owner's discretion.

This document is deliberately short. The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) covers the same ground at greater length, and we hold ourselves to its spirit.
